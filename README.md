# Home Designer

A browser-based home floor plan designer with a live 3D view. All measurements are in millimetres.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # open the printed URL (http://localhost:5173)
```

To build a static copy you can open from any web server: `npm run build` (output in `dist/`).

### With Docker

```bash
docker compose up -d --build    # http://localhost:8080
PORT=3000 docker compose up -d  # use a different port
docker compose down             # stop it
```

The image builds the app and serves it with nginx. Your plans are stored in your browser and in the files you save, not in the container, so it's safe to rebuild or remove the container.

## What you can do

- **Walls**: Wall tool (W). Click to place corners. To make a wall an exact length, type the length in mm and press Enter. Wall lengths are clear lengths, not counting the part that runs into the walls a wall joins (the panel also shows the centre-to-centre length). Click any wall's length label to type a new length; the wall's end point moves and joined walls follow. While a wall is selected or being dragged, green labels show the clear distance to every parallel wall on each side: the nearest in solid green, further ones dashed. Click one to set that gap exactly. Each wall has its own thickness and height.
- **Rooms**: Room tool (B). Drag a rectangle to create 4 walls and a floor in one go. The Floor tool (A) outlines a floor of any shape. Each room shows its internal measurements (the clear floor size between wall faces) along every side, its internal width × depth, and its floor area inside the walls. You can turn these off under “Snapping & display”.
- **Doors & windows**: hinged, double, sliding glass, cavity slider, garage, windows (standard, large, highlight, bathroom) and open archways. Click one, then click a wall. While you place one, or when one is selected, it shows the distance from each edge to the nearest wall on either side. Click a distance to set it exactly.
- **Furniture & fixtures**: kitchen cabinets and appliances, wardrobes, beds, sofas, tables, bathroom and laundry fixtures, office furniture, stairs, columns, a car, and a "Custom block" for anything else. Items snap back-to-wall and sit flush against neighbouring items, which makes kitchen runs and wardrobes quick to lay out.
- **Renovation materials** (select a room): plasterboard for the walls (to the ceiling, less doors and windows) and ceiling, in m² and 1200 mm-wide sheets of the length you choose (2400–6000), plus cornice and skirting in metres and whole lengths (4.8 m / 5.4 m by default), with a waste allowance. Bathrooms, ensuites and laundries get a reminder to use moisture-resistant board.
- **Floor areas & flooring calculator** (Floor tool, A): the side panel lists the total floor area (all rooms, and everything inside the outside walls) and each room's area and floor finish. Below it, a linear metres calculator: pick a material (80 mm hardwood strip, engineered or hybrid boards, 200 × 200 tiles with 1.5 mm grout, 600 × 600, 600 × 1200…) or type a width, gap/grout and waste allowance, and it shows linear metres for each room and in total. Add a length to count boards or tiles too. Each room in the calculator has a tick box (all ticked to start, with a tick-all box in the header); untick rooms to leave them out of the total (e.g. carpeted bedrooms).
- **Product cards**: select an item that's a real product (or click the ⓘ on its catalog tile, or **Details** in its panel) and a card pops up over the corner of the view with its photo, brand, description, key specs and price, plus **View product** and **Visit <brand>** links. Close it with ✕; it stays out of the way while you edit.
- **ABI Interiors** (catalog → ABI Interiors): 1,277 ABI Interiors products you can place — vanities, basins, baths, toilets, kitchen and laundry sinks, mirrors and shaving cabinets, towel rails and heated ladders, showers, bath fillers, tapware and bathroom accessories. Add the kind of fixture, then choose the exact product in its panel (grouped by ABI's sub-categories, with a colour chip and price): it takes ABI's published size, the finish colour (brushed brass, gunmetal, matte black, oak…), a sensible mounting height and a link to the product page. The product list loads only when you first pick one. Spare parts, samples, wastes, tiles and door / cabinet hardware aren't included.
- **Product links**: items modelled on a real product show a **Product** link in their panel to the maker's or retailer's page: the Rochester buffet & hutch, Lombardozzi buffet, Panasonic split system, Pinnacle shelving, VELUX skylights (the page for the chosen model) and Sun Tunnel, and Timberline vanities (the page for the chosen range).
- **Edit**: select anything to edit exact sizes in the right-hand panel. Number fields accept sums like `2400+90` or `2.4m`.
- **Roof**: click Roof in the toolbar and a roof is worked out from your walls. It finds the outline the walls enclose and builds hip, gable (with proper cross-gables and valleys on L, T and U shapes), skillion or flat roofs. Pitch, eaves overhang and height are adjustable, and the roofing can be **COLORBOND® steel** (corrugated, trapezoidal rib or concealed-fix wide rib, in COLORBOND® colours) or **concrete / terracotta tiles** (flat or wave profile, in common tile colours), drawn with ribs or tile courses running down each slope. **Gutters** (Quad, Half Round, Square Line, Fascia / Hi-front or Ogee, in COLORBOND® colours) run along the eaves but not up gable ends, with optional downpipes at corners and the ends of gutter runs; gutters, a COLORBOND® fascia (matches the gutters, or pick its own colour) and the **eave lining** (Villaboard / fibre-cement soffit, any paint colour, or off) follow the whole outline of the roof, mitred at outside and inside corners, and **Level ridges** keeps every wing's ridge at the same height, and the roof updates as you edit the walls. The outside walls need to join up for this to work. Walls a few degrees out of square are allowed for, and **Square up walls** (in Settings or the roof panel) straightens them while keeping corners joined. If walls are at a real angle (e.g. 45°), the roof covers the overall outline instead.
- **Floors, cornices & skirting** (⚙ Settings): flooring for the whole house (timber, hybrid/vinyl plank, 600 × 600 tiles, 600 × 1200 tiles, carpet, polished concrete, each with named colours), a cornice profile (square set, cove 55/90, square-edge, classic ogee, federation) and a skirting profile (shadow line, square-edge 66/90/135, pencil round, bullnose, colonial, lamb's tongue). For timber, hybrid plank and large tiles, **Boards run** sets the direction (left–right, top–bottom, diagonal, or along the room's longest wall), shown as faint board lines in the 2D plan. Any room can override them; the **Wet area preset** gives tiles, a square-set ceiling and no skirting. **Detect rooms** creates rooms from the walls automatically.
- **Brick veneer and other constructions** (⚙ Settings → Outside walls): brick veneer (250 mm), double brick (280 mm), clad frame (130 mm) or concrete block (210 mm). Outside walls thicken outward, so rooms keep their internal size, and the facade follows. Inside walls meeting them stretch along their own line and stay square, and walls that are only partly outside are split so only the outside part thickens. The 2D plan shows the cavity.
- **Doubled-up walls**: the Room tool doesn't draw walls where walls already exist, doors and windows cut through any wall drawn on top of theirs, and Settings shows **Remove doubled-up walls** when it finds any.
- **Solar panels** (catalog → Solar): an array of panels you place over the roof; in 3D it lies on the roof slope on rails (or on a tilted ground frame if there's no roof). Choose the panel (Jinko Solar Tiger Neo 440 W, Trina Solar Vertex S+ 445 W or SunPower Maxeon 7 445 W, sized from their datasheets), rows, columns and portrait / landscape; the panel shows the system size in kW, panel area and weight, and the product card has each panel's specs.
- **Paint each side of a wall**: a selected wall has a paint colour for each inside face, labelled with the rooms that side faces, so the two sides of an internal wall can be different colours (or one side painted on its own). A link sets both sides back to one colour. Changing the general wall colour in Settings still updates any face that used it.
- **Wall linings**: plaster, VJ panelling (full height), VJ wainscot with a dado rail, shiplap or board & batten, for the whole house, per room, or per wall. A selected wall lets you set each face separately (labelled with the rooms it faces), with quick **VJ both sides** buttons.
- **Interior doors** (⚙ Settings → Interior doors): a house style (design, glass, colour and jamb) for every door on an inside wall. Doors you give their own design keep it, and **Apply to all interior doors** brings them back into line.
- **Doors**: proper jambs, either **single** or **double rebated**, with architraves on inside faces. Hinged doors can use designs based on real ranges, grouped by maker: Hume entrance (Savoy, Linear, Newington, Vaucluse, Joinery, Haven), Hume internal (Accent, Accent glazed, Linear, Hayman moulded panel, Oakfield oak veneer), Corinthian internal (Madison, Moda, Motive, Impressions), Corinthian entrance (Madison), Hume Hampton HAM1 (glazed or MDF panel), Hume Haven HAV44 (four lites over a panel, with its matching HAV45 sidelite; translucent, clear, cathedral or grey tint glass), Front Porch Properties (Ivy Willow, Ivy Willow Breeze 2-in-1 with built-in screen, Oaklands, Oaklands Breeze, Arch Ivy Willow) with clear, cathedral or reeded glass (frosted on the Arch) and matching sidelites, plus plain flush and Shaker. Glass can be clear or translucent. Internal doors use 35 mm leaves and entrance doors 40 mm. Single hinged doors can have a **sidelight** on the hinge side, the latch side or both (300–600 mm). The door keeps its size and the opening widens. The model codes shown are examples from each range, so check the maker's catalogue for exact layouts.
- **Timberline vanities** (Bathroom → Vanity (Timberline)): pick the range (Kansas, Bargo, Grange, Henley, Carlo, Nevada, Nevada Plus, Emmett, Colby, Elwood, Ainsworth, Billie, Sutherland House Regency/Farmhouse/Deco/Retro) and a width from 450 to 2100 mm. Then set wall-hung, legs or kickboard; drawers or doors; shaker, V-groove, fluted, flat or curved fronts; above-counter, undercounter or integrated basin; single or double bowl; stone or Silksurface top; and the finish. Check Timberline's catalogue for the exact model.
- **Every Hume door**: the door design list has every model from Hume Doors' entrance and internal catalogues (July 2022), grouped by range — entrance: Haven, Savoy 820 / 1200, Verve, Linear, Joinery, Illusion, Vaucluse, Newington, Regency, Nexus, Vaucluse Premier, Carringbush and Glass Opening; internal: Hampton, Humecraft, Sorrento, Joinery, Linear, Accent, Strata and Moulded Panel (Oakfield, Caprice, Pacific, Denmark, Ascot, Hayman). Each is drawn from the catalogue (glass lites, raised panels, routed grooves) in DuraXP white or timber veneer, and entrance doors with a matching sidelite (e.g. XS3 → XS4, JST1 → JST11, XN5 → XN11) use it automatically when you add a sidelight. The earlier simplified Hume styles are kept under “Hume — quick styles”.
- **Door handles & clearance**: hinged doors have a handle setting: lever, knob, D pull handle, flush pull or none, with its height (1000 mm by default) and finish (satin chrome, brushed nickel, matte black, brushed brass, white). The app checks every hinged door swinging open: if the leaf or a handle would hit a wall or furniture before 90°, the swing shows red in the plan with a cross where it hits, and the door's panel says how far it opens and what it hits, with a **Fix** button that slides the door along its wall just enough to clear. New doors keep clear by themselves when you place them (hold Shift to place one exactly).
- **Stacker doors**: 2, 3 or 4 sliding glass panels on separate tracks that stack to one end. Pick the number of panels and the width of each, and the opening width is worked out for you (panels overlap 50 mm, plus a 50 mm frame each side); or type an overall width and the panels share it. **Hinge / slide other side** changes which end they stack to. In the walk-through they start open (stacked), and E or a click slides them shut.
- **Curtains & blinds**: select a window, sliding or stacker door and choose **Curtains / blinds**: roller blind (light filtering or blockout), Roman blind, venetian blind, sheer curtains, blockout curtains, or sheer + blockout on a double track. Pick the fabric colour, how far they're lowered / drawn (down, half, up / closed, half, open) and, for curtains, floor length or just below the sill. They hang on the inside of the house (for internal walls you can choose the side) and show in 3D, the walk-through and the plan.
- **Garage doors**: single or double, in sectional, sectional with windows, roller, tilt, timber-look or glass and aluminium styles, with Colorbond® colours. The 2D plan shows the ceiling tracks or roller hood.
- **Facade** (▦ Facade button): the outside finish of the house. Choose from face brick, painted brick, render, smooth paint, weatherboard, vertical cladding, fibre cement panels, timber cladding, stacked stone and concrete block, all drawn at real size. Paint, render and cladding use a Dulux® exterior colour palette, and brick, stone, timber and block have their own colours (plus mortar colour). The app works out which face of each wall is outside, so inside faces keep their wall colour, and gable ends under the roof use the facade too. To mix finishes, select a wall and set **Outside finish → This wall only**. Colours are screen approximations, so check a Dulux colour chip before buying.
- **Clearances**: while you place an item (vanity, toilet, cabinet…), and while it's selected, green measurements show the gap from each side to the nearest wall or other item at the same height, in 2D and 3D. Click one on a selected item to set that gap exactly.
- **Add things in 3D too**: pick anything from the catalog and a see-through preview follows the mouse in the 3D view. Doors and windows attach to the wall under the pointer, and furniture snaps to walls and neighbours. Click to place, drag to orbit while placing, Ctrl+click to keep placing, Esc to cancel.
- **Buffet (Lombardozzi, Tas oak)** (Dining): solid Tasmanian oak buffet after Harvey Norman's Lombardozzi, in its 110 / 158 / 207 cm sizes (Size buttons in the panel): drawers across the top, doors below (3 drawers over 4 doors at 207 cm), tapered legs and finger pulls. Depth (450) and height (800) are typical figures, as the retailer's full specs weren't available.
- **Corner sofa**: set **Return on** to Left or Right to flip which side the chaise is on.
- **Banquette seating** (Dining): built-in upholstered bench seats for a dining nook, straight or an L-shaped corner unit (choose which side the return is on). Pick the back style (channel tufted, plain cushion, buttoned, or a timber back with no cushion), the upholstery (bouclé, linen, velvet and leather colours) and the seat height (460 mm suits a standard 750 mm table). The item colour is the panelled timber or painted base.
- **Skylights** (VELUX): a skylight in FS fixed, VS manual, VSE electric or VSS solar opening versions, in the standard sizes (C01 550 × 700 to S06 1140 × 1180; the length runs up the roof, so turn it with R to suit the slope), and a Sun Tunnel (250 or 350 mm tube). Place them anywhere in the plan (they don't snap to walls). In 3D the unit sits on the roof slope; in the walk-through there's a real opening in the ceiling with a daylit shaft (or a round diffuser for the Sun Tunnel).
- **Garage storage**: heavy duty boltless steel shelving after Bunnings' Pinnacle units: 4 tier (1820 W × 540 D × 1830 H), a taller 5 tier (2090 H) and a narrow 900 mm unit. Choose 2–6 tiers and the shelf finish (particleboard, white melamine, galvanised or black steel); the item colour is the frame (matte black by default).
- **Heating & cooling**: split system air conditioners, an indoor wall unit (placed 2.2 m up, with its louvre and display) and an outdoor unit (fan grille, service panel, feet), sized from Panasonic's 8 kW Z series (CS/CU-Z80YKR: indoor 1040 × 244 × 295 mm, outdoor 875 × 320 × 795 mm). Resize them for other capacities.
- **Outdoor**: deck (boards on posts and bearers at any height, with a glass balustrade added at 1 m and above), veranda (skillion roof on posts, falling away from the house), porch (gabled entry roof), pergola and patio paving. They back onto the outside of a wall like other items. Verandas and porches can be set to **Extend the main roof over this**, so the house roof wraps round them as if their outer edge were an outside wall. Set width, depth, height (deck height, or veranda height at the house) and colour in the panel.
- **Garden designer** (🌿 Garden): landscape the block round the house. **Block of land**: pick a shape (rectangle, corner block with a splayed corner, battle-axe, wedge / cul-de-sac, angled rear or irregular), type the frontage and depth, and the block is set out with the house set back 6 m from the street (facing the way the front wall faces), or click out your own boundary. Drag its corners to reshape it, drag a + on a side to add a corner, Alt+click a corner to remove it, or type each side's length in the panel. Each side has its own fence (COLORBOND® steel, timber paling, horizontal slat, picket, glass pool fence or a rendered front wall) with its height and colour; fences stop where a driveway or path crosses the boundary, and the street (verge, footpath, kerb and road) is drawn in front. The panel shows the block's area and the house's site coverage. **Yards**: Make yards cuts the block into front, back and left / right side yards round the house, each with its own lawn or surface. **Garden areas**: lawns, garden beds, veggie patches, driveways, paths, paving and gravel, drawn with straight or curved edges (corners can be curved or sharp: double-click a corner to switch it), or dropped in as a ready-made kidney, round, oval, corner, wavy border or rectangle shape and then reshaped. Each has a surface (soft-leaf buffalo, kikuyu, couch, zoysia or synthetic turf; pine bark, hardwood chip, sugar cane mulch; exposed aggregate, concrete, asphalt, pavers, bluestone, travertine, sandstone; decomposed granite, pebbles, scoria…) and beds and lawns can have edging (steel, brick, sleeper, mowing strip, stone). **Driveway from garage** and **Path to front door** run one straight out to the street. **Plants and features**: 17 Australian natives (kangaroo paw, grevillea, lomandra, dianella, coastal rosemary, bottlebrush, banksia, waratah, lilly pilly hedge…), 15 trees (natives such as a dwarf lemon-scented gum, water gum, tuckeroo and Bangalow palm, plus jacaranda, crepe myrtle, Japanese maple, citrus and olive), 12 vegetables and herbs, raised garden beds (timber sleepers in hardwood, treated pine, cypress, recycled or painted, one to four sleepers high; or corrugated steel modelled on the Birdies range, rectangular or round, in the Original and Tall heights, unpainted or COLORBOND® colours), each planted with mixed vegetables, tomatoes, salad, herbs, strawberries or native flowers; letterboxes (brick pier, post-mounted, pillar); and stepping stones, boulders, retaining walls, pots, bird baths, a fire pit, bench, water tanks, rotary clothesline, shed and compost bin. Plants are sized fully grown; **Just planted / In 3 years / Fully grown** shows them smaller. **Quantities** lists turf, mulch, gravel and pavers (with extra for waste), edging, soil for the raised beds, fencing by type and a plant list. Everything shows in 3D too. Check plant sizes with your nursery, raised bed sizes with Birdies, and fencing, retaining walls and site coverage with your council.
- **Edit in 3D**: drag furniture, fixtures, doors, windows and walls directly in the 3D view. Furniture snaps against walls and other items the same way it does in 2D, and doors and windows slide along their wall or onto another one. Drag a window up or down to change its sill height. To raise or lower furniture (e.g. wall cabinets), drag the blue up/down arrows above the selected item, or Alt/Ctrl-drag it. Page Up/Down nudges the height 10 mm (100 mm with Shift). When something is selected in 3D, green measurements show its distance to the walls (or other furniture) on each side, plus the gaps below and above it. Click one to type an exact value. R rotates and Del deletes. Drag empty space to orbit. This works with the roof on: clicks go through the roof to the door, window or furniture underneath, and the roof turns see-through while something is selected.
- **Measurements shown**: by default the plan shows wall lengths and each room's overall size and area. In ⚙ Settings → Snapping & display you can also turn on each room's side measurements, and the green clearance measurements (gaps to walls and other items while selecting, dragging and placing, in 2D and 3D).
- **Global settings** (⚙ Settings): general wall height, door height and wall colour for the whole house. Every paint colour picker has a **Dulux / Porter's Paints** switch (remembered in your browser). Dulux offers 2,140 colours from dulux.com.au (with each colour's code and LRV where published) and Porter's Paints all 654 of its colours, grouped by colour family, with a colour chip beside each name and a link to the chosen colour's page on the maker's website; the facade's painted finishes use the same switch. Interior colours (walls, ceilings, skirting, doors) can be picked by name from a Dulux® interior range, including popular whites and greys such as Lexicon, Hogs Bristle and Grey Pebble. Skirting and doors also offer timber stains. These colours are screen approximations, so check a Dulux colour chip before buying. Walls and doors that use the general value follow any change, while ones you've set differently (e.g. a half-height wall) stay as they are unless you click "Apply to all".
- **Walk-through** (🚶 Walk in the 3D view): explore the house in first person as a 170 cm tall person (eye level about 1.585 m). W A S D or arrow keys to move, mouse to look (click the view to capture the mouse), Shift to run, Space to jump. You start just outside the front door. Walls, windows and furniture are solid. Doors start open. Look at a hinged door and press **E** (or click) to swing it shut or open again. Closed doors block you. You can also step up onto decks, patios and porches (up to 450 mm) and climb staircases. Ceilings (colour and height in Settings) cover everything inside the walls while you walk, and in Split view the plan shows where you are and which way you're looking. Click a wall to restyle the side you're looking at: an inside face gets its paint colour (Dulux / Porter's) and lining (plaster, VJ, shiplap, board & batten…), an outside face its facade (the whole house, or this wall only); changes show straight away, and **Back to walking** carries on. You can rearrange furniture as you walk: click an item to select it (it's highlighted, shows its measurements and opens in the properties panel), click it again to pick it up, then look where you want it and click to put it down. It snaps to walls like in the editor. R rotates, Del deletes and PgUp/PgDn raise or lower it. Press Esc to free the mouse and edit its size, colour and so on in the panel. The walk-through plays *House Tour* by Sabrina Carpenter (from 0:30) through YouTube's player (a small player in the corner); M pauses it, and **turn off** in the walk-through panel stops it for next time too. It pauses while the shotgun's soundtrack plays. If YouTube asks you to sign in or press play (it does this for anonymous viewers), the player gets bigger and the panel tells you: press Esc and use the player; being signed in to YouTube in that browser usually avoids it. Or choose **use a music file instead** to play an audio file of your own (it stays in your browser). Esc frees the mouse, and Esc again (or **Exit walk-through**) returns to editing.
- **3D**: 2D, Split and 3D views. The 3D view opens with the roof hidden so you can see inside; the **Roof** button shows it. Move around with W A S D or the arrow keys (Q / E to go down / up). Orbit, pan and zoom the camera, click objects to select them, and use "Cutaway walls" to see inside.
- **Save**: Save / Save as writes a `.home.json` file to your computer, and Open loads one. In Chrome and Edge, Ctrl+S saves straight back to the same file. Your current plan is also autosaved in the browser.
- **Render quality** (Quality menu at the top of the 3D view, or ⚙ Settings → 3D display): **Low** turns off shadows and lowers the resolution for slow laptops, **Medium** uses sharper, cheaper shadows, **High** (the default) has soft shadows at full resolution, and **Ultra** adds soft reflections and ambient occlusion (gentle shading where walls meet floors and furniture), which needs a decent graphics card. The setting is saved in your browser, not in the design.
- **More space**: collapse the left catalog or the right properties panel with the ‹ › buttons on their edges, or with `[` and `]`. The choice is remembered.
- **Export**: a PNG of the 3D view, or an SVG of the floor plan.

## Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Home Designer is a design and visualisation tool only: not building, structural or engineering advice, and its measurements and estimates are approximate. The full **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy** and **Brand Disclosure** are in the app (a notice on first visit, and links at the bottom of **⚙ Settings**). The brands featured aren't sponsors: no payments, kickbacks or affiliate links. In short: no accounts, analytics or tracking; designs stay in your browser and your own files (or in a share link, if you choose to send one: the design is packed into the link after the `#`, which isn't sent to the web host); the walk-through music loads YouTube's player.
